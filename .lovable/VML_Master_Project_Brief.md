## 1. The Business

Vintage Marquee Lights is a maker of artisan, handcrafted illuminated marquee-style signs, operating since 2008. The company sells directly to businesses, entrepreneurs, and event rental companies. Custom fabricated signs (wall-hanging, 3D layered, logo signs, mobile vendor) are positioned as artisan and handmade — never referred to as "commercial grade." The 36"/48" stand-up event letters and rental inventory packages are the only products referred to as commercial-grade. Signs are constructed from steel (hand-painted or powder-coated) with 1-inch G3 incandescent globe bulbs set into the channels of each letter or shape, similar in construction to channel letters. They plug in like a lamp — no special electrical work required.

**Critical fabrication constraint:** The channel must be a minimum of 2 inches wide to accommodate the 1-inch bulb centered in the channel. This rule affects font selection and minimum sign sizes and must be enforced in any configurator or quoting tool.

**Construction details:** Steel construction, hand-painted (develops natural vintage patina) or powder-coated (smooth, shiny, weather-resistant). Bulbs are 1-inch G3 incandescent globe (candelabra base, 7W or less; LED compatible). Turn-around time is 4–6 weeks standard for custom fabrication; rush orders available. **Rental inventory packages ship via ocean freight only (~14 weeks total lead time). The detailed breakdown is: 30 days production + ~52 days ocean freight + up to 14 days port-to-door = approximately 96 days (~14 weeks).** Custom signs ship UPS Ground; freight for oversized pieces.

**Brand voice rules:** Never call the bulbs "Edison bulbs" — always say "standard incandescent filament bulbs" or "G3 globe bulbs." Blog posts should be 400–600 words with 3 sections using H2 headers. Always end blog posts with: `@[brand_instagram] — your sign looks incredible. Tag us when you share it!`

  - Up to 48": Add FW powder coat amount × qty × 1.25 (25% markup only).
  - 49"+: $10/sq ft (manual review, do not automate).
  - Custom powder colors require manual review.

**Shipping & Freight Rules:**
- Pricing includes shipping (UPS Ground by default).
- Daisy-chained wiring and a single plug are included and do not trigger freight.
- **Freight Flag = YES** if: Letters are physically connected, raceway/rigid backing/connected bars are used, it's a one-piece assembled unit, a pallet is required, or it exceeds UPS limits (over 165" girth).
- Freight requires manual review. Never auto-calculate freight dollars.

#### Rental Package Payment Terms

All payment details for rental inventory packages. This section is the authoritative reference for any sales, CRM, or customer communication work.

**Default: Full payment due at order.** This is the standard expectation communicated to all customers. Full payment confirms the production slot, starts the timeline immediately, and avoids any delays.

**Payment methods (in order of preference):**

| Method | Tag | Key Details |
|---|---|---|
| Bank Transfer / ACH / Wire | Most Popular | **3% discount applied for full payment by bank transfer.** No processing fees. Funds clear quickly; production starts without delay. Contact Jerrad first for wiring instructions. |
| Shop Pay Installments | — | Customer applies through the Shopify invoice link at checkout. Subject to Shop Pay approval. No surcharge added — customer pays the invoice price as shown. |
| Credit or Debit Card | 3% fee added | Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover accepted via Shopify invoice link. A **3% processing fee is added** to the order total to cover transaction costs. |
| Business or Personal Check | 10-day hold | All checks from new customers are subject to a **mandatory 10-day clearing hold** before production begins. Full payment is required upfront; order does not enter production until the check fully clears. |

**Optional 70/30 split payment (available on request, not the default):**
- **70% due at order** — starts production immediately; holds the production slot.
- **30% due at completion** — due approximately 30 days later, before the order leaves the fabrication facility. The balance must clear before the shipment is released.
- If the 30% balance is paid by check, the 10-day clearing hold applies to that payment as well, adding a minimum of 20 extra days to the total timeline across both payments.
- The 3% bank transfer discount applies to full payment only, not to the 70/30 split.

**Invoicing process:** VML sends a secure Shopify draft order (invoice) directly to the customer's email. No account creation required. Customer opens the link, selects payment method, and completes checkout.

**Air Freight Bridge:** To address the 12–14 week ocean freight wait, VML offers a subsidized air freight starter kit of 4–5 high-demand letters for approximately $500 out of pocket (VML subsidizes the remaining $1,000+ in air freight costs). Delivery: 4–6 weeks. This allows buyers to start booking events and collecting deposits before the full fleet arrives.

**Sample Unit Qualifier:** A single 36" letter at $800, shipped within 7 days. If the buyer purchases a full package within 30 days, the $800 is credited toward their order.

## 4. The Own-It Program

The Own-It program is VML's flagship B2B offer targeting entrepreneurs evaluating marquee letter license programs (Alpha-Lit, Stellar Letters, Event-Lit, Letter & Light Co., Love Lights). The core value proposition is ownership vs. licensing: no franchise fees, no annual royalties, no territory restrictions. Buyers own the inventory outright and keep 100% of every booking.

**Target buyer:** Someone actively evaluating a marquee letter license program, or researching how to start a marquee letter rental business. They are comparing a $25K–$35K license program against VML's ownership model.

**Key sales talking points:**
- Own the inventory outright — no license fees, no royalties, no territory rules
- Commercial-grade 36" and 48" letters built for repeat rentals
- Suggested rental rate: $75–$125 per letter per event; avg "MR & MRS" booking ~$700
- Break-even in approximately 12 months
- Financing via Shop Pay Installments
- Trusted since 2008 — the original marquee light makers
